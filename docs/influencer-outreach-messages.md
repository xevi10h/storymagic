# Influencer Outreach Messages

> Research date: 2026-06-03
> Purpose: ready-to-send outreach copy for potential meapica creator partners.

Contact details are based on public web sources found during research. Recheck before sending because creator contact details change often.

## Outreach Principles

- Do not lead with equity.
- Lead with a personalized digital preview, tracked code, and 60-90 day collaboration test.
- Keep the ask concrete: one preview/reel/story/comment dynamic first.
- Use "personalized story/book" and "children as protagonists"; avoid leading with "AI".
- If the profile is premium or editorial, emphasize quality, reading, and emotional value over ecommerce.
