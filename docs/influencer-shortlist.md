# Influencer Shortlist

> Research date: 2026-06-03

This document tracks potential creator and influencer partners for meapica. It should be treated as a living shortlist, not a final hiring decision. Follower counts and engagement data can change quickly and must be rechecked before outreach.

## Selection Criteria

Best-fit profiles should have:

- Parent, family, education, children's literature, reading, creativity, or family plans audience.
- A credible voice with parents, grandparents, teachers, or gift buyers.
- Content style compatible with premium, warm, handcrafted positioning.
- Ability to drive comments, preview requests, checkout starts, and sales.
- Willingness to accept tracked codes/links and performance-based economics.

Avoid profiles that:

- Are mostly lifestyle/fashion with only incidental motherhood content.
- Depend heavily on exposing children for engagement.
- Ask for equity or a large fixed fee before proving commercial traction.
- Have high follower counts but low engagement or poor comment quality.

## Outreach Recommendation

Start with 20-30 profiles from Tier 1 and Tier 2. Do not lead with equity.

Best first offer:

- Free personalized digital preview.
- Unique affiliate code.
- 15-20% first-purchase commission.
- Bonus after 25, 50, and 100 attributed sales.
- Optional partner/advisor conversation only after a 60-90 day traction trial.

Best test campaign:

**"Comenta el nombre y lo que más le gusta a tu hijo/a; creamos 5 inicios de cuento personalizados."**

This creates comments, allows preview generation, and tests whether the creator's audience actually wants the product.
