# Ad video research (2026-10-27)

Asked by the owner after seeing Runway ads. Web research only; Meta Ad Library and TikTok Creative Center were not
readable headless, so competitor formats come from third-party libraries and published cases.

## Runway vs what we have

- Documented: Gen-4.5 (first frame only, no last frame), Gen-4 References (up to 3 images), Aleph 2.0 (video-to-video
  edit of real footage), Act-Two (performance capture), Characters (real-time avatars), Agent 2.0 for campaigns. No
  dedicated "ads" product flow; "Runway makes ads" is Agent/Workflows marketing.
- API: $0.01/credit. Gen-4.5 12 cr/s, Gen-4 Turbo 5 cr/s, Aleph 2.0 28 cr/s, Act-Two 5 cr/s. Plans 15/35/95 $ a month.
- Verdict: duplicates Higgsfield for our case. Only Aleph (fix light/background on real creator footage) is new. Try by
  API (~10-20 $) if needed, no subscription.
- Rule kept: AI never regenerates the book interior; use real rendered pages or real footage.

## What converts in the category

- Wonderbly (Motion library): woman to camera with an occasion CTA (27 s), hand unboxing + page flip (15 s), cover
  slideshow (12 s), UGC interior demo (29 s). No price on the creative.
- Hooray Heroes (8x agency case): peaks are gift-reveal reaction videos, hook in the reaction, always an occasion.
- Pattern: real reaction or unboxing, an occasion or deadline, 12-30 s. Ours are 55-60 s.

## Platform guidance

- TikTok (official): value prop in the first 3 s, 9:16, sound on, captions, safe zone, real people and lo-fi look,
  3-5 very different creatives per ad group.
- Meta (third-party sources): hook in 2-3 s, 15-30 s, captions always; Reels safe zone keep top 14 %, bottom 35 %,
  sides 6 % clear. Fewer ad sets, more genuinely different concepts.

## AI disclosure

- Meta: "AI info" label, detects external tools by C2PA. EU AI Act art. 50 applies from 2026-08-02: synthetic realistic
  people must be disclosed. TikTok AIGC ad label reported mandatory since 2026-07-21 (agency source, not confirmed).
- So photoreal AI people scenes must say they are AI-generated ("Escena recreada" alone is not enough).

## Five concepts (no invented testimonials or stats)

1. "Subes una foto de tu hijo… y pasa esto" (15-20 s): real screen recording of the preview generating, real pages
   with the face, price "IVA incluido", CTA. No AI people.
2. "Así llega su cuento a casa" (15-25 s): real hands unboxing a printed book, cover with the name, page flip with
   sound, price, CTA. No AI.
3. Creator gives the book to the child (20-30 s): "Le he hecho un cuento donde el protagonista es él", real reaction,
   real page inserts, CTA.
4. Reyes deadline (15-20 s): face close-up in an illustration, three real spreads lightly animated, real book in hand,
   real order-by date from delivery.ts, price.
5. Photo to illustration (12-18 s): "¿Lo reconoces?", consented real photo morphs into the illustrated child (first+last
   frame model via Higgsfield), three adventures, CTA.
