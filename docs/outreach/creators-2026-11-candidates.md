# Creator candidates for the affiliate programme (researched 2026-10-23)

New accounts to invite to the Meapica creator programme (10 % off for followers with the creator's code, 10 % of the
ex-VAT amount for the creator, panel at meapica.es, optional gifted book). **Nobody here has been contacted.**
Everyone listed in `creators-2026-10.md`, `creators-2026-10-messages.md`, `influencer-shortlist.md` and the media
lists is excluded.

## Method

- Discovery: web search (Spanish lists, city family-plans guides, teacher/author accounts, Modash/HypeAuditor
  snippets) and the Llista.cat API (all 20 categories, ~1,200 Catalan creators, snapshot 2026-10-01).
- Verification: every row below except @mamaeducativa and the TikTok-only @cori_entrepetits was opened on Instagram
  on 2026-10-23, **logged out** with a throwaway headless profile (58 profile views, 6 s apart; no blocks). Followers and bio come from that view. "Last post" is the newest
  post date in the grid (pinned posts ignored). One read of three hashtag grids with the @meapica_books session gave
  no usable owners (grids hide the author), so it was not repeated.
- **Not checked:** individual captions. "Why it fits" is based on bio, highlights and the image descriptions of the
  newest posts, not on reading a specific post about Reyes or Sant Jordi, except where a dated post is named.
  Engagement (likes/comments) is not visible logged out: the signal column gives posting cadence and, for Catalan
  accounts, Llista's monthly views where it has them. Check the last three posts before writing to anyone.
- Priority: 1 = write first (in the sweet spot, children or children's books on camera, active, a parent/teacher
  voice like the creators who said yes in October); 2 = good fit with a caveat; 3 = reserve.
- Flags: **UGC** = bio offers paid UGC; **seller** = sells own books/materials (may want stockist deal, not a post);
  **paid?** = signals of paid-only collabs.
